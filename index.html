SIM 01

Cut-sequence optimizer

A cutter has to visit every pierce point. The order decides how much time it spends travelling instead of cutting. Compare the order the points were drawn in against a nearest-neighbour route and a 2-opt improvement of it, then drag any point and watch the routes re-solve.

Synthetic points on a 600 x 360 mm sheet. Drag a point with mouse or touch.

Order

Rapid time assumes 5,000 mm/min rapid feed. Pierce and cut time are the same in every order, so only travel changes.

SIM 02

GD&T position zone

A position tolerance is a circular zone, and with the MMC modifier it grows as the hole gets bigger. Drag the measured hole centre (or focus the view and use the arrow keys, Shift for bigger steps) and change the actual hole size to see whether the part passes.

Scale: the ring is about 0.1 mm across. The hole itself is drawn as a marker, not to scale.
